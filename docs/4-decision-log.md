# Decision Log

Running, **append-only** log of decisions made during planning and implementation that aren't fully
captured in the formal specification documents. Check this before starting work. Append here when a
decision is made that affects other work; never rewrite history — supersede with a new dated entry.

Each entry: a dated, **bold lead sentence** stating the decision, followed by the rationale — what was
decided, why, and the consequence for other work. Group entries under headings (Architecture, Scope &
Strategy, Repo & Tooling, etc.) as they accumulate.
